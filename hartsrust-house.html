Hartsrust House

Hunting accommodation for up to 17, with a trophy-lined dining and braai room for the whole party.

Sleeps 17

The heart of the farm for your hunting party

Hartsrust House is a spacious base for your Karoo hunt, full of character and traditional South African hospitality. The main living, dining and braai room is hung with trophy mounts and has a long table for the group after a day in the veld.

17Guests in total
13In the main house
4In the attached flatlet

Sleeping arrangements

  • Two bedrooms in the main house, each sleeping 3
  • A loft room in the main house sleeping 6
  • A flatlet with space for 4 guests
  • Three-quarter beds throughout

Bathrooms

  • Full bathroom in the main house (bath, shower, toilet)
  • Separate bathroom building with 2 showers and 2 toilets
  • En-suite bathroom in the attached flatlet
