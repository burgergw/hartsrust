Terms & conditions

Booking terms

Booking, billing & payment

  • A non-refundable booking deposit of R30,000, together with a completed booking form, is required to secure your booking
  • The person or company making the booking is invoiced for the entire trip and is responsible for settling the account in full. Accounts are not split
  • No discount is given for cash, due to the additional risk and cost of handling
  • A VAT invoice is provided. All prices exclude VAT

Booking form

Confirm your hunt

Download and print the booking form, fill in your details and email the completed form to arnosteenkamp@yahoo.com. Your booking is confirmed once the R30,000 deposit has been paid in full.

Minimum booking (hunting season)

  • Hartsrust House: minimum 8 guests
  • Goodhope: minimum 6 guests
  • A minimum of 4 nights and 3 hunting days per booking

Because there are a limited number of hunting weeks in the season, these minimums are charged for every booking, even if fewer guests or nights are booked.

Injured game

  • Injured and lost animals are an unfortunate reality of hunting. All animals the guide deems injured are charged at full price
  • An animal is treated as injured if there is a clear sound of bullet impact, a clear response to the shot (jumping, dropping, etc.) or blood spoor is found
  • A bullet strike anywhere on the animal, including the horns, counts as impact
  • Every effort is made to track and recover an injured animal, for up to 90 minutes, after which it is deemed lost
  • A freshly wounded animal found during the day's hunting that isn't claimed by an individual hunter is added to the group account

While we do our utmost not to change these prices, conditions and circumstances change, and we reserve the right to adjust pricing if necessary.
