General Terms & Conditions

VAT

  • All prices exclude VAT.

Booking, Billing and Payment

  • A non-refundable booking deposit of R30,000, together with a completed booking form, is required to secure the booking
  • The person / entity making the booking will be invoiced for the entire trip / hunt and is responsible for settling the account in full. Accounts will not be split.
  • No discount will be given for cash due to the additional risk and cost of handling.
  • A VAT invoice will be provided.
  • Minimum no. of guests per booking:
    • Hartsrust House - minimum 8 guests
    • Goodhope - minimum 6 guests
  • Minimum no. of days / nights per booking:
    • A minimum of 4 nights and 3 hunting days per booking is required to ensure a successful and enjoyable hunting experience.

Please note that due to the limited number of hunting weeks available in the season, the above minimums will be charged for every booking, regardless of whether the actual number of guests and/or nights booked is below these minimums.

Injured Game

  • Due to the nature of the activity injured and lost animals are an unfortunate reality.
  • All animals deemed by the guide to be injured will be charged at full price.
  • For the sake of clarity, an animal will be assumed to be injured if there is:
    • a clear sound of bullet impact, or
    • a clear response of the animal to the bullet impact (jumping, dropping etc), or
    • blood spoor is located
  • Every effort will be made to track and locate the injured animal - up to a maximum time of 90 minutes, at which point it will be deemed to have been lost
  • Bullet strikes anywhere on the animal will be deemed to be impact - including the horns
  • If, during the course of the day's hunting, a freshly wounded animal is identified and not claimed by an individual hunter, it will be added to the group account.
