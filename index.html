Welcome to Hartsrust

Hunting specialists, with lodging built around the hunt

Hartsrust is a dedicated hunting farm in the South African Karoo, established in 2015 by Arno Steenkamp on a conservation-through-hunting model.

Across rivers, bush, open veld and koppies we run guided plains game hunts for hunters who take the craft seriously: long-range hunting, walk & stalk and traditional voorsit jag, with more than 20 species on the farm, from springbok and kudu to sable and roan.

Everything on the farm supports the hunt. There's a shooting range from 100 m to 1 km, our own butchery at Die Slaghuis, and two farm houses that sleep hunting parties of up to 26. Whether you're organising an annual hunting trip with friends or hosting a corporate group, we plan the days around you.

Group & corporate hunts

Hunting trips for groups and corporate getaways

Group hunting is what we do best. Book one or both farm houses for your hunting club, a group of friends or a corporate team, and we'll plan the hunting days, range time and evenings around your group.

  • Sleep up to 26: Hartsrust House (16) and Goodhope (10)
  • Hunting bakkie, guide and tracker, cool boxes and lunch included in the day fee
  • Self-catering, or full catering with continental breakfast, lunch and dinner
  • A 100 m to 1 km range, with rifle rental
  • Clay pigeon shooting and game drives for non-hunters
  • Game processed on site at Die Slaghuis, ready to take home

Species

Plains game, including sable and roan

Rivers, bush, open veld and koppies support a wide range of plains game, from springbok in four colour variants to sable and roan, two of the most sought-after antelope in the Karoo.

  • Springbok
  • Kudu
  • Gemsbok
  • Blue, black & golden wildebeest
  • Sable & roan
  • Lechwe & waterbuck
  • Blesbok & red hartebeest
  • Burchell's zebra & warthog

Outside hunting season

Off-season Karoo farm stays

The hunting season runs from 1 March to 30 September. From October to February, our farm houses are open to non-hunting guests who want the open landscapes, star-filled skies and quiet of the Karoo, at our standard accommodation rates.

  • Self-catering, or full catering with continental breakfast, lunch and dinner
  • Wi-Fi, DSTV, ice and firewood included, serviced daily
  • Clear Karoo night skies for stargazing
