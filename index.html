Discover the Beauty and Adventure of the Karoo

Hartsrust Family Farm is a working hunting farm in the Karoo, South Africa, offering unforgettable guided hunting safaris in-season and peaceful farm-house getaways year-round — scenic landscapes, starry skies, and true Karoo heritage.

Welcome to Hartsrust

Whether you're joining us for a guided hunting safari or simply want to escape into the quiet of the Karoo, Hartsrust offers a genuine, working-farm experience. Stay in one of our two farm houses — Hartsrust House or Goodhope — and enjoy wide-open landscapes, unforgettable starry skies, and warm Karoo hospitality.
