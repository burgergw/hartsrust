The Karoo Experience

A legacy of conservation, wide open landscapes, and true Karoo hospitality.

A Legacy of Conservation

Over a decade ago, Arno Steenkamp established Hartsrust as a hunting farm built on a conservation-through-hunting model. Today the farm spans over 45,000 acres of pristine Karoo land, with terrain ranging from rivers and bush areas to open veld and koppies.

Visitors can encounter Springbuck in multiple colour varieties, along with Gemsbuck, various Wildebeest species, Lechwe, Sable, and Roan — a genuine wildlife haven in the heart of the Karoo.

A Wildlife Haven

From rivers and bush areas to open veld and koppies, Hartsrust's varied terrain is home to an impressive diversity of plains game — making every drive across the farm a chance to spot something new.

The Journey Through the Karoo

Explore multiple habitats across the farm — from riverbanks to open veld — before winding down with a sunset drive and stargazing under some of the clearest night skies in the country.

Taste of the Karoo

No Karoo visit is complete without the food. Enjoy roosterkoek, kerrie mince, vetkoek, potbrood, hearty liver dishes, and fresh springbuck fillet tartare — a true taste of the region.

Beyond the Hunt

Clay pigeon shooting, mountain biking, nature photography, and game drives are all part of the Hartsrust experience — whatever brings you to the Karoo.
