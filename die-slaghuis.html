Our Abattoir

Die Slaghuis — professional on-site game processing.

Hartsrust provides a complete hunting experience, including an on-site abattoir where the game you hunt is professionally processed. Our skilled team ensures that the meat is handled with care, providing you with high-quality cuts and products.

This service adds convenience and value to your hunting adventure, allowing you to take home a true taste of the Karoo.
