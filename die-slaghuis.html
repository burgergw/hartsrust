Die Slaghuis: On-Site Game Processing

Our own butchery on the farm, so the game you hunt goes home as high-quality meat.

From the veld to your freezer

Professional processing, without leaving the farm

Hartsrust runs a complete hunting operation, including an on-site butchery where the game you hunt is professionally processed.

Our skilled team handles the meat with care, turning your animal into high-quality cuts and products, from steaks and mince to biltong. It adds real value to a hunting trip: you take home a true taste of the Karoo, and groups can have everyone's game processed in one place.

Services

Game processing & trophy preparation

Choose how your meat is processed: mince, patties, braaiwors, cheese grillers, smoked wors, backstraps, biltong, droëwors and more. Please enquire about the options.

  • Slaughter of game (head off, feet off, internals removed, skin on), included in the day fee
  • Meat processing into cuts, mince, wors and biltong
  • Skin removal, if the carcass isn't processed through our butchery
  • Shoulder mount, skull mount and flatskin preparation
  • Taxidermy and tanning arranged separately
  • Only sheep fat is used in our minced products
  • Game biltong and game droëwors available
  • Karoo lamb, butchered and packaged
