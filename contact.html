Your enquiry

What to include

To give you an accurate quote quickly, let us know:

  • Your preferred dates and number of days
  • How many hunters and non-hunters
  • The species you're after
  • Your preferred hunting style
  • Goodhope, Hartsrust House or both
  • Self-catering or full catering, and whether you need rifle hire or game processing
  • Whether you're hunting, or visiting off-season (October to February)

Booking terms

  • A non-refundable booking deposit, with a completed booking form, secures your booking
  • Hunting bookings: minimum of 8 guests at Hartsrust House or 6 at Goodhope, and at least 4 nights and 3 hunting days
  • The person or company making the booking is invoiced for the whole trip. Accounts are not split
  • No discount for cash. A VAT invoice is provided, and all prices exclude VAT
  • Complete the booking form below and email it back to confirm your booking
  • The full price list document and information guide are available on request
