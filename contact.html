Your enquiry

What to include

To give you an accurate quote quickly, let us know:

  • Your preferred dates and number of days
  • How many hunters and non-hunters
  • The species, or slam, you're after
  • Your preferred hunting style
  • Goodhope, Hartsrust House or both
  • Whether you need rifle hire, meals or game processing

Booking terms

  • A non-refundable booking deposit of R20,000 secures your dates
  • All prices are in ZAR and exclude VAT
  • The full price list document, information guide and booking confirmation form are available on request
