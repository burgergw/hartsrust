Where to Stay

Two beautifully appointed houses, designed for comfort during your hunting adventure or a relaxing Karoo getaway.

Both houses at Hartsrust are equipped with everything you need for a relaxing and memorable experience. We take care of the details so you can focus on leisure, not logistics.

  • Spacious outdoor areas with braai facilities
  • Firewood and ice provided
  • Indoor braai and dining room areas
  • Full kitchens with gas freezers and refrigerators
  • Daily cleaning service
  • Full DSTV and Wi-Fi
  • Solar-heated geysers for hot water
  • Extra blankets provided for winter months
  • All linens and towels included
