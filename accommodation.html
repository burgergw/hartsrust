Groups & corporate getaways

Book both houses for your group

Bringing a hunting club, a group of friends or a corporate team? Book Hartsrust House and Goodhope together for up to 26 guests. Hunting bookings are for a minimum of 8 guests at Hartsrust House or 6 at Goodhope, and at least 4 nights. Self-cater in the full kitchens, or choose full catering with continental breakfast, lunch and dinner. The day fee applies to every guest, hunting or not.

Off-season & non-hunting stays

Stay outside hunting season

The hunting season runs from 1 March to 30 September. From October to February, non-hunting guests are welcome in both farm houses at the same accommodation rates: R1,000 per person per night self-catering, or R2,000 per person per day fully catered.

Ask about off-season dates

Ready to book your stay?

Self-catering accommodation is R1,000 per person per night, or R2,000 per person per day with full catering. Rates apply to all guests, including children aged 5 and younger.
