Long-Range, Walk & Stalk and Voorsit Jag Hunts

Hunting experiences for every hunter's preference, from seasoned marksmen to beginners.

Hunting styles

Choose how you hunt

Hartsrust's mix of open veld, koppies, bush and rivers suits several styles of hunting. Stick to one, or combine them over a multi-day trip.

Long-range hunting

Take on the open plains and koppies with long, precise shots. Sight in at our range first, from 100 m out to 1 km.

Walk & stalk

The thrill of tracking plains game on foot. Bush walking and waterbank setups also give you opportunities at waterbuck and lechwe.

Voorsit jag

A more traditional approach: the South African sit-and-wait hunt, where patience and a well-placed shot do the work.

Range & equipment

Shooting range from 100 m to 1 km

Hone your skills at our on-site shooting range, with distances from 100 m right out to 1 km. It's the place to confirm your zero before a long-range hunt, and a good way to start a group trip.

Rifles, including ammunition, are available to rent by the day if you'd rather not travel with your own firearm.

After the shot

Processing & services

  • Slaughter of game (head off, feet off, internals removed, skin on), included in the day fee
  • On-site meat processing at Die Slaghuis, our butchery
  • Shoulder mount, skull mount and flatskin preparation
  • Taxidermy and tanning arranged separately
  • Rifle rental, including ammunition
  • Hunting bakkie, cool boxes, guide and tracker included in the day fee

Accommodation & catering

Stay on the farm during your hunt

Our two guest houses, Goodhope and Hartsrust House, sleep up to 26 hunters and guests and are serviced daily. Lunch is included in the day fee, and you can self-cater or choose full catering with continental breakfast, lunch and dinner.
