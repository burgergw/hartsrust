Goodhope Farm House

Hunting accommodation for up to 14, with braai areas and a fire pit.

Sleeps 14

A rugged, comfortable base for your hunt

Goodhope blends rustic charm with contemporary comfort in the Western Cape's rugged Karoo landscape. Expect vast open spaces, a fire pit for the evenings and panoramic views across the farm.

6Min. guests (hunting)

House specs

  • Sleeps up to 14 guests
  • 2 bathrooms: 2 showers, 2 toilets
  • Off-grid solar power
  • Wi-Fi and DSTV
  • Serviced daily

Facilities

  • Full kitchen with stovetop, refrigerator and freezer
  • Indoor braai dining room
  • Outdoor braai area and fire pit
  • Outdoor seating on the stoep

Booking

  • Self-catering or full catering
  • Hunting season: minimum 6 guests, 4 nights and 3 hunting days
  • Hunting season: the day fee applies to every guest, hunting or not
  • Off-season (October to February): non-hunting guests welcome at our standard accommodation rates
