Goodhope Farm House

Hunting accommodation for up to 14, with a separate flat, braai areas and a fire pit.

Sleeps 14

A rugged, comfortable base for your hunt

Goodhope blends rustic charm with contemporary comfort in the Western Cape's rugged Karoo landscape. Expect vast open spaces, a fire pit for the evenings and panoramic views across the farm.

14Guests in total
11In the main house
3In the separate flat
2Bathrooms

Sleeping arrangements

Bedding includes 7 three-quarter beds and 2 sets of bunk beds, which gives flexibility for a range of group sizes and compositions, from a family hunt to a full hunting party.

Amenities

  • Two bathrooms with toilets and warm showers
  • Full kitchen with stovetop, refrigerator and freezer
  • Indoor braai dining room
  • Outdoor braai area and fire pit
  • Outdoor seating on the stoep
  • Full DSTV and Wi-Fi
