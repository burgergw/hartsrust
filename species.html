Plains Game Species at Hartsrust

More than 20 huntable species and colour variants in the Karoo, including sable and roan.

On the farm

Varied terrain, varied game

Hartsrust's terrain runs from rivers and bush areas to open veld and koppies, and supports an impressive diversity of plains game. Plan your hunt around the species you're after, or a mixed bag for the freezer.

Sable & roan

Sable and roan in the Karoo

Alongside the classic Karoo plains game, Hartsrust is home to sable and roan antelope, two of the most sought-after antelope in South Africa. Get in touch to discuss availability and the hunt you have in mind.
