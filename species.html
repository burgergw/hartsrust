Plains Game Species at Hartsrust

More than 20 huntable species and colour variants on 45,000 acres of Karoo.

On the farm

Varied terrain, varied game

Hartsrust's terrain runs from rivers and bush areas to open veld and koppies, and supports an impressive diversity of plains game. Plan your hunt around a single trophy, a mixed bag for the freezer, or a full slam.

Premium species

Sable and roan in the Karoo

Alongside the classic Karoo plains game, Hartsrust holds sable and roan antelope, two of the most sought-after trophies in South Africa. Trophy fees for both are on request, so get in touch to discuss availability and the hunt you have in mind.
